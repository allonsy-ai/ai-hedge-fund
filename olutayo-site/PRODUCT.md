## Platform

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS, single page, no build step. Shared as a private preview link first; can be hosted later on GitHub Pages, Netlify or Vercel.

## Users

- Recruiters and hiring managers filling Product Manager / Product Owner roles, mainly in Dublin, Ireland and the EU, often in fintech, payments, data and AI. They scan fast, usually from a LinkedIn or email link, and want to judge fit in under a minute.
- Founders and teams looking for freelance or contract product help (MVPs, prototyping, analytics, AI product work).

## Product Purpose

Personal site for Olutayo Solana. It presents a Product Manager who ships fintech and data products with measurable outcomes, and makes it easy to get in touch for either a full-time role or freelance work. Success means a visitor understands the fit within seconds and makes contact (email, phone or LinkedIn).

## Positioning

The mix is product judgement plus the ability to build and test quickly.

1. Outcomes first: every claim is tied to a real number or deliverable from the CV.
